## doodle brand
This project is doodle coffee's website. For any visual or written work, follow `brand/BRAND_GUIDELINES.md` (the `doodle-brand` skill has the summary). Use the logo SVG in `brand/assets/logo/`, the tokens in `brand/tokens/tokens.css`, periwinkle #4F57D6 on white, Fraunces italic for headlines and DM Mono for everything else, all lowercase.

Product truth: PRODUCT.md. Business planning lives outside this repo in ../strategy and ../ops.
