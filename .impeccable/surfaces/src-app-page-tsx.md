# Surface: launch page (/)

Mode: persuade. Visitor: someone who met doodle at the cart, on Instagram, or from a bag's QR code, usually on a phone. Job: understand what doodle is and leave an email to hear when the cart rolls out. Proof on hand: the brand itself (wordmark, three drawings, roast names, bag lineup). No photos, cart dates, prices or reviews exist; none are invented. Email provider undecided: form posts to a placeholder endpoint.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the page is a day, not a catalog. The three roasts arrive as three times on a morning-to-night clock (*daydream* at 7:40am, *the usual* at 11:15am, *late show* at 3:30pm), refusing the hero-over-three-product-cards template.

OWN-WORLD: white paper ground, one periwinkle ink for every letter and line; mist only for the cart band. Pastel arrives only as one chisel highlighter under a single word. Fraunces italic (soft, wonky, lowercase) for headlines and roast names, DM Mono lowercase for everything else. Single-stroke line drawings at large scale with no boxes, circles, shadows, pills or gradients. Periwinkle button with 10px corners; inputs are a periwinkle underline, as on the bag's "roasted on ____" line.

STORY: doodle is pretty good coffee from a Philly cart that you take home → it fits every part of your day and isn't fussy → leave an email; we'll say when the cart is out.

FIRST VIEWPORT: wordmark top-left (~150px), "say hi →" top-right. Headline "coffee for every part of your day." at headline scale, left, with the butter highlighter under "day". One line of sub-copy. Email field + "tell me when" button inline beneath. Across the bottom of the viewport is the day line: one hand-wobbled periwinkle stroke spanning the width, with three time marks (7:40am daydream · 11:15am the usual · 3:30pm late show) as anchor links to their sections.

FORM: "a day in three cups", 3rd on my ordered list of 7; seed:6ca58cf4dd83f842. Signature interaction: each roast's drawing draws itself in one stroke as its section enters view (stroke-dashoffset), and a small dot on the hero day line slides to the time you are reading. Reduced motion shows the finished drawings.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
